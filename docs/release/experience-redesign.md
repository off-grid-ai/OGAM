# What's new: a calmer Off Grid AI

Everything still runs on your phone. It's quicker to read now, and it looks like one app from Home to Settings.

## When you open the app

**Home shows you what you can do.**
- A row of cards you can swipe: describe a picture and watch it appear, talk instead of typing, ask your own files, turn a line into a clip, or borrow your Mac's bigger models. Things you haven't tried come first.
- The row moves on by itself every 8 seconds, waits whenever you touch it or come back to the app, and holds still while a sheet is open.
- Clipboard and Sync each fit on one line, and the bell and crown line up with the title.

## When you chat

**One line under each reply.**
- Time, how long it took, read it aloud, details and more, as icons on one line. Details tells you where it ran and how fast, in plain words.

**Quick settings that open where you tap.**
- The menu grows out of its button. Every row is easy to hit, and values read as words: On, Off, Auto.

**One place for model settings.**
- A chat's settings link to Model settings instead of copying them. Model settings is now tabs (General, Text, Image & video, Voice), with nothing folded away.

## When you keep work together

**Projects you can read at a glance.**
- Every row is the same height and shows how many chats and files it has, and when you last used it ("Last week", "2 weeks ago").
- A project shows what it's for, a New chat button, and one "What it knows" card for its instructions and files. You can still add a file or paste text.

## When you pick a model

**The model sheet opens straight away.**
- The text model sheet opens in about half a second. It used to take nearly 3.
- Your Mac or a server with hundreds of models shows five, plus Show all.
- Your model has one check, the same in every sheet.

**Picks made for your phone.**
- Fast, Balanced and Best sit side by side at the top of each tab, and they don't change while you look.
- Speech models say when they're English only, so two models are never both called "Small".

**Labels you can trust.**
- Models that make pictures no longer say they "see images", and a photo model is never called anime.
- Tags never get cut off. When a model does three or more things, you see named icons.
- On Models, "In use" and "On phone" sit in the same place on every row.

**Downloads match Models.**
- The same tabs in the same order, Documents included.

## When you look at what you made

**Pictures and films open full screen.**
- Tap one and it fills the screen on dark. More gives you Share or Save, Delete and the details.
- Videos stay in the Gallery, with your stories, clips and cast at the top.

## Settings

**Every list reads the same.**
- One short line per row, so lists keep one height. Section names sit above their cards.
- Remote servers and your devices show a coloured dot beside the word: Connected, Checking or Not answering. Last seen reads as "5 minutes ago".
- Storage counts what you have ("2 models", "3 chats"). Device tiers say what each phone can run.
- "Built by Wednesday" is quiet grey, and it shows once.

## The look, everywhere

- Cards are glass, so the moving field shows through, the way Off Grid AI looks on your Mac.
- The same 24-point edges on every screen and sheet.
- The same search field, with its icon and a clear button, on every Models tab.
